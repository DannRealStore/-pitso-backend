PITSO - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to PITSO. PITSO is a prototype food ordering app for Pi Network, built by a Pioneer from the Philippines. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect and what we do NOT collect.

1. What We Collect

When you use PITSO in Testnet, we may collect:

  • Your Pi username - to know who placed the order
  • Your order details - what meal you ordered
  • Your Pi payment information - only through Pi Network's official payment system. We do NOT see your Pi password or passphrase.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Your Pi passphrase or wallet password
  • Your Pi private key
  • Your Facebook password
  • Your bank account or credit card details

Never share your Pi passphrase with anyone, including us. PITSO will never ask for it.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Process your Testnet order
  • Verify your Pi payment through Pi Network's approve and complete system (Zero Trust - we verify, we don't just trust)
  • Improve PITSO

4. Pi Network Payments

All Pi payments are processed by Pi Network. PITSO does not store your Pi wallet information. Every transaction is verified on Pi's system before we confirm your order.

5. Testnet Status

PITSO is currently a prototype in Testnet. There is no physical fastfood restaurant yet. All payments are Testnet Pi for testing purposes only. No real Pi or real money is used.

6. Third-Party Links

PITSO contains links to Facebook, YouTube, and Pi Network. When you click those links, you will leave PITSO and follow that platform's own privacy policy.
